Your next AI assistant will message you first. Set the rules now.

Last week, Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet: an agent that watches the apps you connect, sends a morning brief, and messages you when something needs attention.

It asks before it sends a message, pays, signs or deletes anything. Today it is only for US Pro and Max users, on a waitlist.

The real shift is not the tool. It is that AI moves from something you use to something you delegate to.

And delegation is a skill most of us learned with people, not software.

Before you hand work to any proactive agent:

→ Start with one read-only routine, like a morning brief, for a week
→ Write a short brief: what to watch, what counts as urgent, what it may draft
→ Review weekly what it flagged, what it missed, and what you approved without reading

The full version, with a delegation brief you can copy and the current limits: https://ellemmatos.github.io/ellemmatos/ai/2026/10/26/ai-assistant-will-message-you-first.html

What is the first task you would delegate to an AI agent that works in the background?
