Back from maternity leave, and AI had moved on. Now what?

I returned to work after maternity leave this year. In AI, a few months is a long time. New tools, new features, new ways of working in the team.

The first reflex is to read everything. That does not work. There is too much, and most of it does not change your job.

What helps is catching up on purpose:

→ Ask the team to show you how they work with AI today, not to send you links
→ List the three changes that affect your work, and ignore the rest for now
→ Try one new tool or feature for 20 minutes a day, on a real task
→ Ask AI to summarise what changed in a tool you use, then check the official notes

Leave also gives you something useful: distance. You notice the habits the team stopped questioning. That is a good place to start a conversation.

A career break is not a reset. It is a restart with a fresh view.

What helped you most when you came back from a long break?
