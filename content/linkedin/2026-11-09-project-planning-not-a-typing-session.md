Project planning should not be a typing session.

In many planning meetings, one person shares a screen and types. Everyone else watches. The real questions come at the end, when people are tired: what is risky, what depends on what, what fits.

AI is good at the typing. People are needed for the deciding. So split the two:

→ Before the meeting, turn each requirement into draft tasks with acceptance criteria
→ Ask AI for dependencies, risks and open questions
→ Tell it to flag what is unclear instead of guessing
→ Ask the people who know the work to correct the drafts in advance

Then the meeting is only about decisions: open questions, disputed risks, estimates and what the team commits to.

The team still estimates and commits. AI does not know which system is fragile this month or who is on holiday.

The full method, with prompts and a checklist: https://ellemmatos.github.io/ellemmatos/ai/2026/11/09/project-planning-not-a-typing-session.html

How much of your last planning meeting was typing?
