AI as first reviewer, humans as final sign-off.

Anthropic published early results from its drug development partnerships last week. In one case, Claude reviewed 1,061 pages of documentation. It found all 11 issues human reviewers had found, plus 7 they had missed. Human review time fell 89%.

Human review still caught a minor error in a document Claude drafted.

For project teams, the lesson is the order of work:

→ Let AI do the first full read
→ Keep a named person for the final sign-off

What document would you hand to an AI first reviewer?
