Your team has AI tools. Nobody works differently. Here is why.

I coached ~15 developers, Scrum Masters and Team Leads from their first prompt to working with AI on their own.

The licences were never the problem. Habits, confidence and trust were.

What made the difference was simple: someone sat with each person for their first real task. Not a training session. Their own work, together, for 30 minutes.

In the slides:

→ The 4-stage adoption path
→ How to run that first session
→ Why meetings are the best place to start
→ The rules to agree before you scale
→ 5 mistakes that kill adoption
