With AI agents, the bottleneck is now your attention.

Anthropic published a study last week on how work is changing inside the company. About 80% of interactive Claude session time is spent waiting for human input.

For project teams, that changes planning. If agents produce faster than people can review, the queue moves to the reviewer.

→ Plan review time like you plan build time
→ Make one person own each agent's output

How much time does your team plan for reviewing AI work?
