You do not always need senior people. You can grow them.

When a new team needs a lead, the first reflex is to look for someone who has done it before. Sometimes that person does not exist, or is not available.

I have trained Scrum Masters with little prior experience and mentored Team Leads. What made the difference was not talent. It was structure:

→ A shared playbook, so nobody starts from a blank page
→ Short workshops on one practice at a time
→ Real responsibility early, with someone to call when it gets hard
→ A fixed coaching rhythm, not help only when things go wrong
→ Feedback on what they did, not on who they are

It takes more of your time in the first months. But it repeats: the same structure helped us scale from 7 teams to more than 22.

Growing people is also how more women get into technical leadership. Many are ready long before they feel ready.

Who gave you your first real responsibility?
