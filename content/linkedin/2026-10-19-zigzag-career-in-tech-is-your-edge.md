Your zigzag career in tech is not a weakness. It is your edge.

My path: electronics, IT support, the Brazilian Army, SharePoint and .NET development, delivery facilitation, and now project management for AI-enabled teams.

On paper, that can look like drift.

Many people with a path like this hide old roles or start interviews with "I know my background is unusual, but…". The problem is not the path. It is that the reader has to find the connection alone.

Try this before your next interview or profile update:

→ For every step, write one problem you solved and one skill you carried forward
→ Turn it into one sentence: "I started in X, moved through Y, and that is why I can Z"
→ Add two results with numbers or scope

In a market that changes every few months, a zigzag path proves you can learn fast and deliver.
