Claude can now join your meetings. Decide what it may do.

Anthropic launched Claude Meetings in beta last week. Claude joins a Google Meet call as a visible participant. It writes notes and action items. It can also start a doc, a whiteboard or a lookup in a connected tool while the meeting is still running.

That is more than a note taker. A note taker records decisions. A participant can act on them.

Before your team turns it on, agree on five things:

→ Consent: everyone on the call agrees before Claude joins
→ Which meetings: planning and design reviews, yes; 1:1s and sensitive topics, no
→ What it may do: notes and drafts, plus a short list of allowed tools
→ Who owns the output: one person checks the notes and sends them
→ A review date: try it for two weeks, then adjust

One detail matters: for connected tools, Claude asks the first time, and a yes applies from then on.
