I am going back to school on AI project management. Hold me accountable.

I use AI in my projects every week: meetings, planning in Jira, tests, code review, documentation.

But using AI to deliver a project is not the same as delivering an AI project. I have not led the delivery of an AI product yet. That is my next step: becoming a project manager for AI initiatives.

My starting point: before project management, I was a senior .NET and SharePoint developer. I can discuss architecture with tech leads as a peer.

The plan:

→ The free PMI course "PMI Essentials: Seven AI Project Patterns"
→ Two weeks, six short sessions
→ One post per pattern, every week: what I learned and where I see it in real work
→ Then the free introduction to PMI's CPMAI certification

I will only write about what I have actually studied.

Why I am writing the plan out, with the full series: https://ellemmatos.github.io/ellemmatos/ai/2026/11/23/back-to-school-ai-project-management.html
