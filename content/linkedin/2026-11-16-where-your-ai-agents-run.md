Where your AI agents run is now a choice.

Last week, Anthropic opened a private beta of self-hosted Claude Managed Agents. Teams can run the agents, credentials and session history in their own cloud or on-premises, and choose where requests to the model go.

For teams in regulated sectors, "where does our data live?" often blocks AI pilots for months. Options like this change that conversation:

→ Bring security and compliance in before the pilot
→ Ask what must stay inside, and what may leave

Who in your company answers that question today?
