Before you scale AI, check your settings against a baseline.

Anthropic added a security center to Claude Enterprise last week, in beta. It compares an organisation's settings with Anthropic's recommended configuration, control by control, and admins can download a report for auditors.

For teams rolling out AI, the lesson is simple:

→ Know which settings you have today
→ Compare them with a recommended baseline
→ Fix the gaps before you invite more people

Who in your team owns the AI settings today?
