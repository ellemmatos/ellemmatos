AI will not give you work-life balance. But it can buy back 5 hours a week.

Balance depends on workload, people and what happens at home. No tool fixes that.

But most weeks hide hours of small, repeated tasks: preparing meetings, writing updates, sorting messages, planning the week.

The mistake is trying everything at once, or asking AI for help only when stuck. Time comes back from small routines you repeat every week.

Try this for two weeks:

→ List your recurring tasks and how long they take today
→ Pick two routines, like a 5-minute meeting brief and a status update from your notes
→ Track the minutes, keep what works, drop the rest

Then decide where the saved time goes, and put it in your calendar. If you do not claim it, your week will.

"5 hours" is a target to test, not a promise. One real hour back is still a win.
