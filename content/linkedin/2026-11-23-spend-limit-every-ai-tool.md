Give every AI tool a spend limit.

Last week, Anthropic launched linked billing on the Claude Platform, in beta. Tools like Asana and Linear can now bill Claude usage to a customer's own account. The customer approves each tool, sets a monthly spend limit, and can unlink it at any time.

For AI projects, cost is not a one-off licence. It grows with use. So:

→ Agree a spend limit per tool before the pilot starts
→ Name who watches the usage every month

Who owns the AI budget in your project?
