## Introduction

Most teams already have AI tools. Few teams actually work differently because of them.

As Lead Scrum Master and Project Manager, I worked with remote teams spread across several regions and time zones, most of them working in English as a second language. I coached ~15 people, from developers to Scrum Masters and Team Leads, from their first AI prompt to working autonomously.

I turned what worked into a free, practical playbook.

## What the playbook covers

- **A four-stage adoption path:** Start, Guide, Autonomy, Scale
- **Use cases across the delivery cycle:** meetings, sprint planning and Jira, prototyping, documentation of legacy code, testing and code review
- **Coaching people:** how to run the first session, follow up, and handle resistance
- **Guardrails:** data rules, human review, and the EU AI Act AI literacy context
- **How to measure adoption** and a **30-60-90 day rollout plan**
- **Ready-to-use prompt templates** for meetings, Jira and code documentation

## The key lesson

The gap is not the technology. It is habits, confidence, process and trust.

Adoption happens when someone sits with each person for their first real task, then steps back. One small personal win convinces more than ten slides.
